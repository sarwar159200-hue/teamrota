# TeamRota Phase 30

## Professional organization chart
The organization chart now includes search, department filtering, zoom, fit-to-screen, professional cards, clear primary hierarchy connectors, and visible dotted/functional reporting connections.

## Standard rotation patterns
- 1 Week ON / 1 Week OFF — 7/7
- 2 Weeks ON / 2 Weeks OFF — 14/14
- 28 Days ON / 28 Days OFF — 28/28
- 6 Weeks ON / 2 Weeks OFF — 42/14
- Full Time — Sunday to Thursday ON; Friday and Saturday OFF

Admin and HR can assign any pattern and use Manual Rota Override for exceptional dates.
